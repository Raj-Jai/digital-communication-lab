## Concepts Covered

- Line codes and their spectra.
- Pseudo-noise sequence generation and autocorrelation.
- BPSK and QPSK modulation/demodulation.
- Clock and data recovery (edge detection, PLL).
- Simulation-based verification with schematics and waveforms.
